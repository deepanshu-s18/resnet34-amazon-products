## ⚠️ Demo vs Trained-Model CAMs

> **These images were generated with a randomly-initialised model.**

Random-weight CAMs do NOT show meaningful localisation — they prove the implementation is correct (hooks fire, backward runs, CAMs are normalized) but the heatmaps are arbitrary.

Trained-model CAMs with semantic meaning will be committed after ABO training (E-003) completes.
See [EXPERIMENT_LOG.md](../../EXPERIMENT_LOG.md) for status.
